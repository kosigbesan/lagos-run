# LAGOS RUN 🇳🇬

A bright, cartoonish endless runner through everyday Lagos — dodge the Danfo, jump the potholes, and chop the Jollof.

Play it in your browser (no download): **https://kosigbesan.github.io/lagos-run/**

## The loop

RUN → COLLECT → AVOID → SCORE → **HUSTLE**

## How to play

- **◀ ▶** or **A/D** — change lane
- **▲ / W / Space** — jump
- **Mobile** — swipe left/right/up, or use the on-screen buttons

## Collect 🍚🥤🍢🥥💰🎧

| Item | Points |
|---|---|
| 🍚 Jollof | +10 |
| 🥥 Coconut | +10 |
| 🥤 Zobo | +15 |
| 🍢 Suya | +20 |
| 💰 ₦100 | +50 &nbsp;·&nbsp; **+₦100 to your wallet** |
| 🎧 Headphones | shield |

## Avoid 🚌🏍️🕳️🚧

Danfo · Okada · Pothole · **BRT Bus** · **Flood** · **😎 Area Boy** (he'll pinch your ₦)

And watch for **🍲 Mama Put** on the roadside — she'll feed you.

## Levels

1. **YABA** 🌆 — learn the streets
2. **IKEJA** 🏙️ — okadas & BRT, faster traffic
3. **LAGOS ISLAND** 🌧️ — rain, flood, heavier traffic

## The ₦ hustle

₦ persists across runs. Spend it in the **Shop** to unlock characters (Okada Rider, Mama Put, Danfo Driver, Area Boy, Traffic Warden, Jollof Mascot) and upgrades (Magnet, Head Start Shield, Second Chance, ₦ Doubler).
